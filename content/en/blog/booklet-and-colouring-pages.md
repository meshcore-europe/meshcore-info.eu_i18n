You can now print 2 things to tell people near you about the mesh: a
[booklet](/press-kit/#booklet) that explains MeshCore, and 9
[colouring pages](/wallpapers/#colouring). Both are free, so print as many as
you like.

## A booklet to hand out {#booklet}

Hand it to a visitor at your stand, or to the owner of a roof you would like
for a repeater. On 1 folded A4 sheet, it explains what MeshCore is, what it
costs and whether it is allowed. A drawing shows how a message hops from roof
to roof.

Write your name or your group in the empty box on the back. The code next to
it opens Local groups in the booklet's language.

Print it on both sides, flipped on the short edge, at 100%, and fold it once.
The booklet comes in 33 languages, and the press kit offers the one you read
the site in. Spot a mistake in yours? Tell us on
[Discord](https://discord.meshcore-info.eu/), or report it on GitHub with the
[translation form](https://github.com/meshcore-europe/meshcore-info.eu/issues/new?template=translation-bug.yml).

## Pages to colour in {#colouring}

Each page is an A4 PDF with black lines on white, for children, a class or
your group's stand. A cat on a roof reaches for a radio wave, and a circuit
board has chips as big as buildings. On Doodle in numbers, warm colours show
where the beam of an antenna is strong and cold colours where it is weak.
Apart from our web address the pages have no words, so they work in every
language. Print one, or [all 9 in 1 PDF](/media/colouring/colouring-pages.pdf).
