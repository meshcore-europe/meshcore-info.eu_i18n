Since 4 November 2026, [Meetings](/meetings/) lists when local groups meet,
next to the map of [Local groups](/community/). You see who runs the mesh near
you, and when you can meet them.

## Find your group {#groups}

Your local group knows the radio settings of your area and the channels people
use. Ask them, in your own language. On 22 November 2026, Local groups lists
77 groups in 28 countries. Germany has 24 of them, the Netherlands 6. Every
group is public, and we check each link before we add it.

On the map, a circle marks the rough area a group covers and an open dot is a
group for a whole country. Not sure which group covers your town? Search by
postcode under the map.

## See when it meets {#meetings}

Anyone can join a meeting: in person, online or on a channel on the mesh
itself. The IoT meeting in Kiel is at Toppoint and online at the same time.
The Hunebedronde in the north of the Netherlands meets every Sunday at 10:30
on the channel #hunebedronde.

Each meeting has an Add to calendar button. Or subscribe to a calendar link
for one group, for your country or for all of Europe. Your calendar app then
adds new meetings on its own. The link works before a group lists its first
meeting, too.

The map on Local groups marks a meeting in person in the next 30 days with a
square. Each group card shows its next meeting, and the home page shows the
next 3.

## Add your group or your meeting {#add}

Is your group missing? [Add your local group](https://github.com/meshcore-europe/meshcore-info.eu/issues/new?template=add-community.yml)
with its name, its area and its links. PortaMesh around Trier and MeshCore
Franken joined this way on 21 and 22 November 2026.

On 22 November 2026, Meetings lists 7 upcoming meetings from 6 groups, 5 of
them in Germany. The other 71 groups have none listed yet. Does yours meet?
Fill in the form on [Meetings](/meetings/). A club or a hackerspace can list
its meeting too. The form shows your next dates straight away, then opens a
GitHub issue with your answers filled in. A repeating meeting stays listed for
6 months. Send the form again to keep it longer.

No GitHub account, or a question about a group or a meeting? Ask on our
[Discord](https://discord.meshcore-info.eu/).
